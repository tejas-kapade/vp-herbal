How We Craft Our Remedies

We do not mass-produce. Every batch is slow-cooked following traditional Ayurvedic texts.

1

Handpicked Raw Herbs

We source pure, sun-dried herbs directly from trusted cultivators, ensuring zero artificial additives or preservatives.

2

Slow Sun-Steeping & Boiling

Following classical Taila Pak Vidhi, oils and herbs are simmered slowly over wood-fire for hours to extract maximum medicinal essence.

3

Fresh Bottling & Quality Check

Filtered naturally without harsh chemical clarifiers and hand-bottled in sterilized containers ready for your home.
